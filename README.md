Mark of the Gods is a fantasy mod of Quake 4. Changes include:
-6 new stats (Vitality, Vigor, Strength, Swiftness, Sorcery, Wisdom)
-Weapons rethemed into fantasy weapons (swords and magic)
-Upgrade screen at end of a level
-5 non-combat spells
5 equip able items that affect stats; 2 can be equipped at a time 

Stats:
The stat vitality affects the maximum amount of health you have. Vigor affects how fast your health regenerates.
Strength scales the damage of strength-based weapons (Axe, Aether Greatsword, Zweihander)
Swiftness affects player movement speed and the speed of weapons that scale with Swiftness (Short Sword, Axe, Spear)
Sorcery affects scaling of sorcery-based weapons, either making them stronger, more potent, or have more inflictors (Aether Greatsword, Super Sparks, Ice Rod, Fireball, Oblivion Disintegrator, Lightning Storm)
Wisdom makes the non-combat spells more powerful

Weapons:
Blaster -> Dull Dagger
Machine Gun -> Short Sword
Shotgun -> Axe
Hyperblaster -> Ice Rod
Grenade Launcher -> Aether Greatsword
Nailgun -> Zweihander
Railgun -> Spear
Rocket Launcher -> Super Sparks
Dark Matter Gun -> Oblivion Disintegrator
Napalm Gun -> Fireball

Non-Combat Spells:
Use the console (ctrl alt `) to cast wisdom spells
Heal: command = "cast_heal" --- heals player; amount is affected by Wisdom stat
Flash: command = "cast_flash" --- gives player super speed, time is affected by Wisdom stat
Morrowind Jump: command = "cast_mjump" --- allows player to jump really high like that one Morrowind spell (survivability is not guaranteed); height is affected by Wisdom stat
Magic Siphon: command = "cast_msiphon" --- gives player magic weapon buffs in exchange for health; potency, time, and health sacrificed is affected by Wisdom stat
Strength Siphon: command = "cast_ssiphon" --- gives player strength weapon buffs in exchange for health; potency, time, and health sacrificed is affected by Wisdom stat

Items:
Use the console (ctrl alt `) to equip and unequip items
Ring of Health: commands = "equip_roh", "unequip_roh" --- boosts player's Vitality and Vigor stats
Bunny Hood: commands = "equip_bh", "unequip_bh" --- slightly boosts player's Swiftness stat; gives a bigger boost to player speed
Warrior Medallion: commands = "equip_wm", "unequip_wm" --- boosts player's Strength stat
Mage Seal: commands = "equip_ms", "unequip_ms" --- boosts player's Sorcery and Wisdom stats
Mark of the Gods: commands = "equip_motg", "unequip_motg" --- greatly boosts all stats - is the Ultimate Gift bestowed upon the player, the Prophet

At the end of a level, there is a screen the player clicks that loads the next level and randomly upgrades one of the 6 stats by 3 points.
